# Post-MVP: Research visit timeline

## Goal
Researchers can compare how a place and its evidence changed across field visits.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Visits have a stable ID, date, title, and optional context.
- [ ] A chronological timeline filters the active collection and selects its observation.
- [ ] Unassigned observations remain discoverable without an invented date.
- [ ] Date ordering and timezone display are consistent and tested.

## Out of scope
- Calendar invitations, reminders, and team scheduling.

## Constraints
- Keep observed_at intact and visit association optional.
